# Claim-to-source check: tex/intro.tex and tex/background.tex

Scope: every sentence that carries a `\cite{}` and every sentence that names a prior system or author. Lines starting with `%` and the macros `\gtan{}`, `\kaiming{}` and `\yongzhe{}` are ignored. `\rev{}` is treated as plain text.

**Sources read**
- Full text:
  - assess_civ: NDSS'23 PDF. Page numbers are the printed pages 1–15.
  - rlbox: USENIX Security'20. Printed page = PDF page + 697.
  - huang_sok_attack_surface_device_drv_iso: arXiv 2412.16754v1, pages 1–18.
  - sok-compartmentalization: arXiv 2410.08434v1, pages 1–20.
  - civscope: pp. 33–40.
  - dui: author copy. It has no proceedings page numbers, so sections are given.
  - ksplit: OSDI'22, pp. 613–631.
  - sud: USENIX ATC'10 PDF, read in this check.
  - lim2024_kernel_compart_sok: arXiv 2404.08716, read in this check.
- Abstract or secondary only, marked "abstract-level":
  - microdriver+: the Rutgers abstract via search. The full-text hosts csa.iisc.ac.in and scail.cs.wisc.edu are blocked from this session.
  - invariant_infer_empirical_eval: Crossref abstract.
  - Ferrante87PDG: Crossref abstract.
  - microdrivers and Mao11LXFI: search abstracts.
  - The rest: titles plus how Huang SoK, KSplit and the compartmentalization SoK describe them.

Verdicts:
- **SUPPORTED**: the source says it.
- **PARTLY**: the source supports part of the sentence, or the sentence drops a hedge or overstates.
- **NOT SUPPORTED**: the source does not say it.
- **abstract-level plausible**: the sentence fits the title or abstract, but the full text was not read.

Every replacement is the same length as the original or shorter. I checked this with a script; the one-sentence fix in B19 also rewrites the next sentence and is shorter than the two together.
